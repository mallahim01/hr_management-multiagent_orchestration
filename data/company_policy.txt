ACME Corporation – Employee HR Policy Document
================================================
Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Version: 2.1

---

SECTION 1 – LEAVE POLICY
─────────────────────────

1.1 Annual Leave
   • All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
   • Leave accrues at 1.67 days per month.
   • Unused leave (up to 10 days) may be carried forward to the next year.
   • Leave requests must be submitted at least 3 business days in advance except in emergencies.

1.2 Sick Leave
   • Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year.
   • A medical certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days.
   • Sick leave cannot be carried forward to the next year.

1.3 Casual Leave
   • 8 days of casual leave are provided per year for personal emergencies.
   • Casual leave must not exceed 3 consecutive days.
   • Advance notice must be given where possible.

1.4 Maternity and Paternity Leave
   • Maternity Leave: 26 weeks fully paid for the first two children.
   • Paternity Leave: 5 days of paid leave within 1 month of childbirth.

1.5 Bereavement Leave
   • 5 days for immediate family (spouse, children, parents, siblings).
   • 2 days for extended family (grandparents, in-laws).

1.6 Public Holidays
   • ACME observes all national public holidays.
   • If a public holiday falls on a weekend, the following Monday is given off.

1.7 Leave Without Pay (LWP)
   • Employees may apply for LWP after exhausting entitled leave.
   • LWP requires HR manager approval.

SECTION 2 – WORK FROM HOME (WFH) POLICY
─────────────────────────────────────────

2.1 Eligibility
   • Employees who have completed their probation period (6 months) are eligible for WFH.
   • WFH is a privilege, not a right, and may be revoked based on performance.

2.2 WFH Entitlement
   • Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week.
   • Approval from the line manager is required in advance via the HR portal.

2.3 Responsibilities
   • Employees must be reachable and responsive during standard working hours (9 AM – 6 PM).
   • VPN must be used when accessing company systems remotely.
   • Productivity standards must be maintained equivalently to office work.

2.4 Equipment
   • The company provides a laptop for WFH use.
   • Internet connectivity costs are the employee's responsibility unless otherwise agreed.

---

SECTION 3 – REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
──────────────────────────────────

3.1 Travel Reimbursement
   • Business travel must be pre-approved by the department head.
   • Air travel reimbursement is limited to economy class unless the journey exceeds 6 hours.
   • Local travel (taxi, ride-share) is reimbursed with valid receipt.
   • Mileage for personal vehicle use is reimbursed at the standard government rate.

3.2 Meal Allowance
   • On approved business travel: up to $50 per day for meals.
   • No meal reimbursement for WFH days or regular office days.

3.3 Expense Claims
   • Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of incurrence via the HR portal.
   • Claims submitted after 30 days will not be processed.
   • All claims require original receipts or digital equivalents.

3.4 Client Entertainment
   • Must be pre-approved by the department head.
   • Limit: $150 per event.

---

SECTION 4 – PERFORMANCE REVIEW POLICY
───────────────────────────────────────

4.1 Review Cycle
   • Formal performance reviews are conducted twice a year: in June and December.
   • Mid-year reviews are advisory; year-end reviews determine bonuses and increments.

4.2 Rating Scale
   • 5 – Exceptional
   • 4 – Exceeds Expectations
   • 3 – Meets Expectations
   • 2 – Needs Improvement
   • 1 – Unsatisfactory

4.3 Bonus Structure
   • Rating 5: 20% of annual CTC as bonus
   • Rating 4: 15% of annual CTC
   • Rating 3: 10% of annual CTC
   • Rating 1–2: No bonus

---

SECTION 5 – CODE OF CONDUCT
─────────────────────────────

5.1 Professional Behavior
   • All employees must maintain respectful and professional conduct.
   • Harassment (sexual, racial, or otherwise) is strictly prohibited.
   • Violations may result in immediate termination.

5.2 Confidentiality
   • Employees must not disclose confidential company information to third parties.
   • NDAs are signed at time of employment.

5.3 Conflict of Interest
   • Employees must disclose any financial or personal interest in vendors or clients.
   • Outside employment requires prior written approval from HR.

5.4 Use of Company Assets
   • Company devices and internet must not be used for personal gain or illegal activities.
   • Software installed on company machines must be approved by IT.

---

SECTION 6 – OFFBOARDING POLICY
───────────────────────────────

6.1 Notice Period
   • Junior employees (< 2 years): 1 month notice.
   • Senior employees (> 2 years): 2 months notice.
   • Notice may be bought out by either party.

6.2 Exit Process
   • Exit interview is mandatory.
   • Return of all company assets within 3 days of last working day.
   • Full-and-final settlement processed within 45 days of last working day.

6.3 Leave Encashment
   • Accrued annual leave (max 30 days) is encashed at the time of exit.

---

SECTION 7 – REFERRAL POLICY
─────────────────────────────

7.1 Employee Referrals
   • Employees may refer qualified candidates for open positions.
   • Referral bonus: $500 for junior roles, $1,000 for senior roles.
   • Bonus is paid 90 days after the referred candidate joins.

---

SECTION 8 – HEALTH AND WELLNESS
─────────────────────────────────

8.1 Health Insurance
   • All employees and their immediate dependents are covered under company group health insurance.
   • Coverage: Up to $50,000 per family per year.
   • Pre-existing conditions are covered after a 2-year waiting period.

8.2 Mental Health
   • Anonymous counselling sessions are available via the Employee Assistance Program (EAP).
   • Up to 6 free sessions per year.
