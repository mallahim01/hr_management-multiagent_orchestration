ACME Corporation - Benefits and Wellness Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Version: 2.0

SECTION 1 - HEALTH INSURANCE

1.1 Coverage
   - Medical insurance covers the employee, spouse and up to 3 children.
   - Annual coverage limit is 1,500,000 PKR per family.
   - Parents may be added at an additional employee-paid premium.

1.2 Enrolment
   - New joiners are enrolled from their first day; no waiting period applies.
   - Dependant changes must be reported within 30 days of the life event.

SECTION 2 - RETIREMENT

2.1 Provident Fund
   - The company contributes 8.33% of basic salary to the provident fund.
   - Employee contributions are matched up to an additional 5%.

2.2 Vesting
   - Company contributions vest fully after 3 years of continuous service.

SECTION 3 - WELLNESS

3.1 Gym Subsidy
   - A monthly gym subsidy of 5,000 PKR is available on production of a receipt.

3.2 Mental Health Support
   - 8 counselling sessions per year are fully funded and strictly confidential.
   - Sessions are booked directly with the provider, not through HR.

3.3 Annual Health Check
   - A funded annual health check is available to employees over 35.

SECTION 4 - REFERRALS

4.1 Referral Bonus
   - A referral bonus of 50,000 PKR is paid for each successful hire.
   - The bonus is paid after the referred employee completes 6 months.
   - Referrals for Director level and above carry a 150,000 PKR bonus.
