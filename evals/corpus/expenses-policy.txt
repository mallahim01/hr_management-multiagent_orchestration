ACME Corporation - Expenses and Reimbursement Policy
Effective Date: February 1, 2026
Version: 4.1

SECTION 1 - GENERAL PRINCIPLES

1.1 Submission Deadline
   - Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred.
   - Claims older than 60 days will not be reimbursed under any circumstances.

1.2 Receipts
   - Itemised receipts are required for every claim above 500 PKR.
   - Credit card statements alone are not accepted as proof of purchase.

SECTION 2 - TRAVEL

2.1 Air Travel
   - Economy class for flights under 6 hours; premium economy above 6 hours.
   - Business class requires VP approval regardless of duration.

2.2 Accommodation
   - Hotel spend is capped at 18,000 PKR per night in metro cities.
   - The cap is 12,000 PKR per night elsewhere.

2.3 Ground Transport
   - Ride-hailing and taxis are reimbursed at cost with a receipt.
   - Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at 45 PKR per kilometre.

SECTION 3 - MEALS

3.1 Daily Meal Allowance
   - 3,500 PKR per day while travelling domestically.
   - 8,000 PKR per day for international travel.

3.2 Client Entertainment
   - Client meals require the client name and business purpose on the claim.
   - Alcohol is not reimbursable.

SECTION 4 - PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT

4.1 Training Budget
   - Each employee has an annual training budget of 150,000 PKR.
   - Unused budget does not carry into the following year.

4.2 Conference Attendance
   - Conference attendance requires line manager approval 4 weeks in advance.
