ACME Corporation - Performance and Compensation Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Version: 2.3

SECTION 1 - PERFORMANCE REVIEWS

1.1 Cycle
   - Formal performance reviews take place twice a year, in June and December.
   - Mid-cycle check-ins are held quarterly and are not formally rated.

1.2 Ratings
   - Ratings run from 1 (does not meet) to 5 (consistently exceeds).
   - A rating of 2 or below triggers a performance improvement plan.

1.3 Performance Improvement Plans
   - A PIP runs for 90 days with fortnightly documented reviews.

SECTION 2 - PROMOTION

2.1 Eligibility
   - Employees are eligible for promotion after 12 months in their current grade.
   - Promotion requires a rating of 4 or above in the most recent cycle.

SECTION 3 - COMPENSATION

3.1 Annual Increment
   - Salary reviews are effective 1 April each year.
   - Increments are linked to performance rating and market benchmarking.

3.2 Bonus
   - The annual performance bonus is capped at 20% of annual basic salary.
   - Bonus is paid in May and requires active employment on the payment date.

3.3 Salary Advances
   - A salary advance of up to 1 month basic may be requested once per year.
   - Advances are recovered over the following 3 payroll cycles.
