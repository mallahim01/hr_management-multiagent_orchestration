ACME Corporation - IT and Information Security Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Version: 5.0

SECTION 1 - ACCOUNTS AND ACCESS

1.1 Passwords
   - Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are rotated every 180 days.
   - Multi-factor authentication is mandatory on all company accounts.

1.2 Access Reviews
   - Access rights are reviewed quarterly by the system owner.

SECTION 2 - DEVICES

2.1 Company Devices
   - Company laptops are refreshed every 3 years.
   - Full disk encryption is mandatory and centrally enforced.

2.2 Personal Devices
   - Personal devices may access email and chat only, through the managed app.
   - Storing company documents on personal devices is prohibited.

SECTION 3 - DATA HANDLING

3.1 Classification
   - Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted.
   - Employee personal data is always Restricted.

3.2 Incident Reporting
   - Suspected security incidents must be reported within 1 hour of discovery.
   - Reports go to security@acmecorp.com and the line manager simultaneously.

SECTION 4 - ACCEPTABLE USE

4.1 AI Tools
   - Approved AI assistants may be used for drafting and code review.
   - Confidential or Restricted data must never be pasted into external AI tools.
