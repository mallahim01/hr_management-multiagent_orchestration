ACME Corporation - Remote and Hybrid Work Policy
Effective Date: March 1, 2026
Version: 2.2

SECTION 1 - ELIGIBILITY

1.1 Qualifying Period
   - Employees become eligible for hybrid work after completing 6 months probation.
   - Contractors are not eligible under this policy.

1.2 Role Suitability
   - Roles requiring physical presence, such as facilities and reception, are excluded.
   - Line managers confirm role suitability annually.

SECTION 2 - HYBRID ARRANGEMENTS

2.1 Standard Allowance
   - Eligible employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week.
   - Requests are submitted through the HR portal at least 1 week in advance.

2.2 Fully Remote Arrangements
   - Fully remote status requires Director approval and a 3-month review cycle.
   - Fully remote employees must attend the office 2 days per quarter.

2.3 Core Hours
   - All remote employees must be reachable between 11:00 and 16:00 PKT.
   - Calendar availability must reflect actual working hours.

SECTION 3 - HOME OFFICE SUPPORT

3.1 Equipment Allowance
   - A one-time home office allowance of 75,000 PKR is available after 6 months.
   - The allowance covers desk, chair, monitor and lighting. Receipts required.

3.2 Internet Contribution
   - A monthly internet contribution of 4,000 PKR is paid with salary.

3.3 Equipment Return
   - Company equipment must be returned within 10 working days of the last day.
