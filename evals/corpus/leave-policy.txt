ACME Corporation - Leave Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Version: 3.0

SECTION 1 - ANNUAL LEAVE

1.1 Entitlement
   - Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
   - Part-time employees receive a pro-rated entitlement based on contracted hours.
   - Leave accrues at 1.67 days per completed month of service.

1.2 Carry Forward
   - Up to 10 days of unused annual leave may be carried into the next year.
   - Carried leave expires on 31 March and cannot be encashed.

1.3 Notice Requirements
   - Requests of 3 days or fewer require 3 business days notice.
   - Requests longer than 5 consecutive days require 15 business days notice.

SECTION 2 - SICK LEAVE

2.1 Entitlement
   - Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.
   - Sick leave does not carry forward and is not encashable.

2.2 Medical Certification
   - A medical certificate is required for absence exceeding 2 consecutive days.
   - For absences over 10 consecutive days, occupational health review is required.

SECTION 3 - PARENTAL LEAVE

3.1 Maternity Leave
   - 26 weeks of fully paid maternity leave for the first two children.
   - 16 weeks fully paid for subsequent children.
   - Employees must give 8 weeks notice before the intended start date.

3.2 Paternity Leave
   - 10 days of paid paternity leave, taken within 3 months of the birth.

3.3 Adoption Leave
   - 26 weeks paid adoption leave for the primary adopter.
   - 10 days for the secondary adopter.

SECTION 4 - OTHER LEAVE

4.1 Bereavement Leave
   - 5 days for immediate family: spouse, children, parents, siblings.
   - 2 days for extended family: grandparents, in-laws, aunts and uncles.

4.2 Leave Without Pay (LWP)
   - LWP may be requested only after all paid entitlement is exhausted.
   - LWP requires HR Manager approval and is capped at 30 days per year.
   - Pension contributions are suspended for the duration of LWP.

4.3 Study Leave
   - 5 days of paid study leave per year for approved professional certifications.
   - Examination fees are reimbursed on passing, up to 60,000 PKR per year.
