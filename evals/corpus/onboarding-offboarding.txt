ACME Corporation - Joining and Leaving Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Version: 3.1

SECTION 1 - PROBATION

1.1 Duration
   - The standard probation period is 6 months from the date of joining.
   - Probation may be extended once by up to 3 months with written reasons.

1.2 Notice During Probation
   - Either party may terminate with 2 weeks notice during probation.

SECTION 2 - ONBOARDING

2.1 First Week
   - IT equipment is issued on day one; system access within 2 working days.
   - Mandatory security and compliance training is completed in week one.

2.2 Buddy Programme
   - Every new joiner is assigned a buddy for their first 90 days.

SECTION 3 - RESIGNATION

3.1 Notice Period
   - Confirmed employees must give 30 days written notice.
   - Director level and above must give 90 days written notice.

3.2 Garden Leave
   - The company may place a leaving employee on garden leave for the notice period.

SECTION 4 - FINAL SETTLEMENT

4.1 Timeline
   - Final settlement is paid within 45 days of the last working day.
   - Unused annual leave is encashed at basic salary rate.

4.2 Clearance
   - Clearance from IT, Finance and the line manager is required before settlement.

4.3 Experience Letter
   - An experience letter is issued within 7 working days of the last day.
