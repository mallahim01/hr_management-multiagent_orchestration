ACME Corporation - Code of Conduct and Grievance Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Version: 1.4

SECTION 1 - PROFESSIONAL CONDUCT

1.1 Standards
   - Employees must treat colleagues, clients and vendors with respect.
   - Harassment or discrimination of any kind results in disciplinary action.

1.2 Conflicts of Interest
   - Outside employment must be declared to HR in writing before it begins.
   - Holdings above 5% in a competitor or supplier must be disclosed annually.

SECTION 2 - GRIEVANCE PROCEDURE

2.1 Raising a Grievance
   - Grievances are raised in writing to the HR Business Partner.
   - HR acknowledges receipt within 3 working days.

2.2 Investigation Timeline
   - A formal investigation concludes within 15 working days of acknowledgement.
   - Complex cases may extend to 30 working days with written notice.

2.3 Appeals
   - Outcomes may be appealed within 10 working days of the decision.
   - Appeals are heard by a Director not involved in the original investigation.

SECTION 3 - DISCIPLINARY PROCESS

3.1 Stages
   - Verbal warning, written warning, final written warning, then dismissal.
   - Gross misconduct may result in immediate dismissal without prior warnings.

3.2 Records
   - Verbal warnings are removed from the record after 6 months.
   - Written warnings remain on the record for 12 months.
